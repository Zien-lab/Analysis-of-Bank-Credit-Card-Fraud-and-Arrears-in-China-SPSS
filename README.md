# 💳 Analysis of Bank Credit Card Fraud and Arrears in China (SPSS)

**📅 Duration**: Nov 2023 – Dec 2023  
**🛠 Tool Used**: IBM SPSS

## 📌 Project Overview

Analyzed credit card fraud and arrears behavior using machine learning and statistical models in SPSS to uncover patterns in customer credit ratings and fraudulent consumption activities in China.

## 🔍 Key Methods & Findings

- **Credit Rating Analysis**:
  - Applied **Support Vector Machine (SVM)** and **C5.0 Decision Tree** models.
  - Identified **age**, **continuous income**, and **vehicle ownership** as the most influential factors for credit score classification.

- **Fraud Behavior Detection**:
  - Used **Apriori algorithm** and **Discriminant Analysis** to discover high-risk consumption patterns.
  - Found that **more than 5 daily transactions** or **frequent overdrafts** significantly increased fraud probability (**99%+ confidence level**).

- **Predictive Modeling for Fraud Detection**:
  - Built:
    - **CART Decision Tree** with **89% accuracy**.
    - **SVM Model** with **86% accuracy**.
  - Identified key thresholds for fraud risk:
    - **5.5 transactions per day**
    - **¥9,600 average transaction amount**
